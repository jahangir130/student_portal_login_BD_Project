BUBT Intake / Section handling

The software no longer assumes Intake 1-200 or Section 1-30 as actual university counts.
Admin enters the real Intake/Batch and Section/Group for the selected program, based on the
current semester routine/department records. Student routine filters list only Intake and
Section values that already have published entries for the selected department.

BUBT official routine portal uses Program, Intake and Section as separate filters:
https://routine.bubt.edu.bd/

Example current Fall 2026 CSE routine data includes Intake 56 with Sections 1, 2, 3 and 7,
and Intake 55 with multiple sections. This is not a complete master list for every program.
Keep Intake and Section data-driven and semester-specific; do not hard-code a global max.
